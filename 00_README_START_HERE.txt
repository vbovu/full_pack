HOW TO USE THIS PACK
====================

1) 01_NaTIS_Learner_Material
   PRIMARY LEARNER STUDY SOURCE.
   Start here for Rules of the Road, Road Traffic Signs, and Vehicle Controls.

2) 02_SARTSM_Technical_Reference
   DEEP TECHNICAL REFERENCE.
   Use this when NaTIS is too simplified and you want detailed sign meanings,
   application, road markings, signals, design details and nuances.

3) 00_Legal_Authority
   LEGAL REFERENCE / FINAL AUTHORITY.
   Use the National Road Traffic Act and Regulations when you need the precise
   legal basis behind a learner rule.

IMPORTANT LEGAL STATUS NOTES
============================

- The original National Road Traffic Act 93 of 1996 PDF is an official Gazette Act,
  but the Act has been amended over time. Do not assume the 1996 PDF by itself is
  a perfectly consolidated statement of every amendment now in force.

- The National Road Traffic Regulations compilation included here is an official
  government-hosted convenience copy. It is NOT labelled here as a guaranteed
  fully consolidated 2026 text. Later Gazette amendments may need to be read with it.

- The 09 Oct 2013 PDF is a specific official Gazette notice. It is not a replacement
  for the entire National Road Traffic Regulations, 2000.

- National Road Traffic Amendment Act 8 of 2024 is stored under 03_Not_Yet_In_Force.
  As checked on gov.za on 31 Aug 2026, its commencement is still listed as
  "To be proclaimed". Do NOT study its changes as if they are already operative.

- Draft / "comments invited" notices are intentionally NOT included in this pack.
  They are proposals, not automatically current law.
